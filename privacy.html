Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Welcome to NEXORA. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website and use our online tools.

1. Information We Process

NEXORA provides browser-based tools for working with text, images, calculations, and other digital tasks. Information you enter into a tool may be processed by your browser to provide the requested result.

Where a tool operates entirely in your browser, the files and content you provide are not uploaded to a NEXORA server by that tool. This may change if we introduce a feature that requires an external service. We will update this policy when our practices change.

Our hosting provider or other technical services may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and server logs, depending on their configuration.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through the website may be used to provide the requested tools, maintain website security, troubleshoot technical problems, and improve the user experience. We do not claim to collect personal information that we do not actually receive.

3. Images and Files

Images selected for conversion are processed locally in your browser by the current image-conversion tool. You should still avoid using public or shared devices for confidential files and verify that any future feature you use explains how it processes your information.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

NEXORA may use cookies or similar technologies if needed for essential website functions, analytics, or advertising. If analytics or advertising services are added, this policy should be updated to identify the relevant services and explain their use of cookies or similar technologies.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertising

We may use third-party providers for website hosting, analytics, or advertising. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, their cookies and related technologies may be used to serve or measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings and requirements.

NEXORA does not currently promise that any particular third-party advertising or analytics service is active. We will update this page to reflect the services actually used.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain and protect our website. However, no website or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

NEXORA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held about you. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Contact us to make a privacy-related request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as NEXORA develops. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
